Method

How an answer gets published

Most legal-AI tools optimise for a confident-sounding answer. XeerHub optimises for a defensible one. Here is the process behind every entry in the library.

01
Source the provision
A researcher locates the exact article in the gazetted text of the relevant law — never a summary, translation, or secondary source.
02
Draft in plain language
The provision is restated in clear English, preserving its legal effect, for readers without formal legal training.
03
Verify against the text
The draft answer is checked line by line against the source article. Anything that cannot be traced to a specific provision is removed, not softened.
04
Publish with citation
Only then does the entry go live — with the law name and article number attached, so it can be checked independently.

Question on file
Can the government seize my foreign investment?
Registered foreign investment is protected from expropriation except in cases of overriding public interest. Where expropriation does occur, the investor is entitled to adequate compensation at fair market value.
Source Foreign Investment Law · Art. 18
What this means for you
You can take this citation to the gazetted text yourself. If the article doesn't say what we say it says, the entry is wrong — and we want to hear about it.

Labour Code
Do I have to give an employee a written contract?
Every employment relationship must be documented in writing. Verbal agreements are not enforceable for key terms such as salary, duration, and job role.
Labour Code · Arts. 16–18
Foreign Investment Law
Can the government seize my foreign investment?
Registered foreign investment is protected from expropriation except in cases of overriding public interest. Adequate compensation at fair market value is required.
Foreign Investment Law · Art. 18
Income Tax Law
What is the Presumptive Tax Regime and who qualifies?
Under Regulation 9-1, PTR applies to residents whose annual turnover does not exceed USD 50,000. Quarterly tax is the higher of USD 37.50 or 1.5% of quarterly turnover. Listed professionals are excluded.
ITR 2025 · Reg 9-1, 9-2 · ITA 2025 · Art. 9
Environmental Law
Is the import and export of charcoal legal in Somalia?
No. Import and export of charcoal is prohibited under the EPMA 2024. Violations carry criminal penalties including fines and imprisonment, and the government shall confiscate materials used in the offence.
EPMA 2024 · Art. 48(1),(5)–(6)

Common questions

Is this legal advice? +
No. XeerHub provides legal information — verified answers that reference official Somali legislation. It is not a substitute for a qualified Somali lawyer. For complex or case-specific matters, always consult a licensed legal professional. XeerHub helps you arrive at that conversation better informed.
Which laws does XeerHub cover? +
XeerHub currently covers 5 laws:

1. Somalia Labour Code — employment contracts, leave, termination, wages, overtime, and worker rights
2. Foreign Investment Law — investor rights, repatriation, registration, and protected sectors
3. Income Tax Act 2025 (ITA 2025) — PIT, corporation tax, withholding taxes, PTR, GIA, and deductions
4. Environmental Law (EPMA 2024) — environmental impact assessments, permits, and compliance obligations
5. Data Protection Act — personal data rights, controller obligations, consent requirements, and enforcement

More law areas are planned. A Somali-language interface is in development.
How many Q&A entries are in the library? +
The library contains 245 verified Q&A entries across all 5 law areas. Every entry is manually reviewed by a legal researcher before publication. Each answer cites the exact article number from the relevant official legislation — no paraphrasing without a reference, no invented provisions.
How accurate are the AI answers? +
The AI answer engine is strictly grounded in XeerHub's verified knowledge base — it does not draw from the open internet or general AI training data. Every AI answer cites the specific article it is based on. For Income Tax questions, the engine references both the ITA 2025 and the Income Tax Regulation 2025 (Reg No. 0142-2025). The AI is instructed never to fabricate provisions, and if no verified information exists for a question, it says so rather than guessing.
