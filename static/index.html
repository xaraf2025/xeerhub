Question on file
Who handles labour disputes in Somalia?
Verified answer
A labour dispute is first submitted to the District Labour Inspector, who attempts to resolve it within 14 working days. If unresolved, a party may refer it to the Regional Labour Inspector. If still unresolved, the matter may proceed to the competent court.
Source Labour Code · Arts. 207–210
What this means

Four questions, four citations

Every entry in the library follows this structure — plain answer, exact source. Nothing published without an article reference.

Labour Code
Do I have to give an employee a written contract?
Every employment relationship must be documented. Verbal agreements place the full burden of proving contract terms on the employer — a written contract is strongly recommended.
Labour Code · Arts. 16–18
Foreign Investment Law
Can the government seize my foreign investment?
Registered foreign investment is protected from expropriation except in cases of overriding public interest. Where expropriation occurs, adequate compensation at fair market value is required.
Foreign Investment Law · Art. 18
Income Tax Law 2025
What is the Presumptive Tax Regime and who qualifies?
PTR applies to residents with annual turnover under USD 50,000. Quarterly tax is the higher of USD 37.50 or 1.5% of quarterly turnover. Listed professionals are excluded.
ITR 2025 · Reg 9-1, 9-2 · ITA 2025 · Art. 9
Environmental Law (EPMA 2024)
Is importing or exporting charcoal legal in Somalia?
No. Import and export of charcoal is prohibited. Violations carry criminal penalties including imprisonment of two to five years, fines, and confiscation of all materials used in the offence.
EPMA 2024 · Art. 48(1),(5)–(6)
