🟢 Labor Law
Do I have to give an employee a written contract?
Yes. Every employment relationship must be documented in writing. Verbal agreements are not enforceable for key terms such as salary, duration, and job role.
Labour Code · Arts. 16–18
🔵 Company Law
Can one person start a company alone in Somalia?
Yes. A single individual aged 18 or over can found a company. The minimum age of 18 is a firm requirement — no one under 18 can be a founding member.
Companies Law · Art. 12
🟡 Investment Law
Can the government seize my foreign investment?
Registered foreign investment is protected from expropriation except in cases of overriding public interest. Adequate compensation at fair market value is required.
Foreign Investment Law · Art. 18
🔷 Income Tax Law
What is the Presumptive Tax Regime and who qualifies?
Under Regulation 9-1, PTR applies to residents whose turnover does not exceed USD 50,000/year. Quarterly tax = higher of USD 37.50 or 1.5% of quarterly turnover. Professionals are excluded.
ITR 2025 · Reg 9-1, Reg 9-2 · ITA 2025 · Art. 9

Common questions

Is this legal advice? +
No. XeerHub provides legal information — verified answers that reference official Somali legislation. It is not a substitute for a qualified Somali lawyer. For complex or case-specific matters, always consult a licensed legal professional. XeerHub helps you arrive at that conversation better informed.
Which laws does XeerHub cover? +
XeerHub currently covers 6 laws:

1. Somalia Labour Code — employment contracts, leave, termination, wages, overtime, and worker rights
2. Companies Law (No. 18/2019) — company formation, directors, shareholders, registration, and dissolution
3. Foreign Investment Law — investor rights, repatriation, registration, and protected sectors
4. Somali Civil Law — contracts, obligations, civil liability, and limitation periods
5. Income Tax Act 2025 (ITA 2025) — PIT, corporation tax, withholding taxes, PTR, GIA, and deductions
6. Environmental Law (EPMA 2024) — environmental impact assessments, permits, and compliance obligations

Additional laws — including Maritime Law and the Revenue Administration Law — are referenced where relevant. More law areas are planned. A Somali-language interface is in development.
How many Q&A entries are in the library? +
The library contains over 240 verified Q&A entries across all 6 law areas. Every entry is manually reviewed by a legal researcher before publication. Each answer cites the exact article number from the relevant official legislation — no paraphrasing without a reference, no invented provisions.
How accurate are the AI answers? +
The AI answer engine is strictly grounded in XeerHub's verified knowledge base — it does not draw from the open internet or general AI training data. Every AI answer cites the specific article it is based on. For Income Tax questions, the engine references both the ITA 2025 and the Income Tax Regulation 2025 (Reg No. 0142-2025). The AI is instructed never to fabricate provisions, and if no verified information exists for a question, it says so rather than guessing.
